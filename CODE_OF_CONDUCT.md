# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Sotto to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any public space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer at **shalahuddinshanto@gmail.com**. Reports
are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, or other
contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or
seriously break it.
