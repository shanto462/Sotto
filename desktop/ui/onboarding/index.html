    Welcome to Sotto

    Quiet answers next to your real work. Sotto looks at the Chrome window you're on, asks Claude about it, and shows the answer in an overlay that stays out of screen shares.

    • Ask about the current window
      Press Control M in Chrome to capture the window and get an explanation, recap, or definition.
    • Ask out loud
      Press Control Shift V anywhere to ask a quick question by voice.
    • Private on screen shares
      The overlay is excluded from screen capture, like speaker notes on a podium.

    • deceive anyone about the source of your work, knowledge, or qualifications;
    • evade proctoring, monitoring, or screen-sharing tools, including by using the hidden overlay where its presence hasn't been disclosed;
    • break an academic-integrity policy, employment agreement, or platform terms, or any law or AI provider's usage policy.

    Good uses include personal learning and productivity, speaker notes during streams or recordings you control, reading help for your own materials, and accessibility aids.

    Connect OpenRouter

    Sotto reaches Claude through OpenRouter. One key covers both answers and voice transcription.

    The key is encrypted in your system keychain and never written to disk in plain text.

    Choose models

    Pick the Claude model that answers and the model that transcribes voice. You can change both later in Settings.

    Running from source
    macOS gives these permissions to the app that launched Sotto (your terminal), not to Sotto. Build the app with npm run build:mac and open Sotto.app so the permissions belong to Sotto.
